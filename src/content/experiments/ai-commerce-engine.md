## Question

What does "AI-powered commerce" actually mean when it's applied to a small business — not Amazon, not Shopify — but a single-location gadgets business with 500 products?

## Hypothesis

Three specific use cases might matter:

1. **Product discovery** — a customer asks in natural language; the system finds the right product from inventory
2. **Intent classification** — is this customer buying, browsing, complaining, or researching?
3. **Pricing recommendations** — based on demand, stock, margin, and competitive signals

Each is a small, well-defined problem. Together, they could meaningfully improve the commerce experience for both sides.

## Status

**Idea.** Not yet prototyped.

## What's Next

- Research existing open-source solutions in the space
- Build a minimal product discovery prototype using Gemini + PrimeShieldHub's catalogue
- Measure whether natural language search meaningfully beats keyword search
